Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

EngFinite Learning (secretstudyroom.com) is a free website where learners practise for the IELTS exam. This page explains what information we keep and why, in plain words.

What we collect

  • Your account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares only your name and email with us. We never see your Google password.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored in a protected form by our login provider. We cannot read it.
  • Your profile (if you fill it in): your full name, phone number, country, division or state, city or district, and an optional street address. Only the site owner can see these details. We use them to know and contact our students.
  • Your practice activity: tests you save or finish, and when you last logged in.
  • Your writing: for students who use Writing Practice, the essays and answers you submit, and for Task 1 the pictures you upload with your own questions.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your progress.
  • To check your writing and give you feedback. Your text (and any picture you upload for Task 1) is sent to an AI service so it can mark your work and write the feedback.
  • To send you emails about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To keep the site safe and stop misuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who helps us run the site

We use trusted services to run EngFinite Learning: Supabase (login and database), Google (the "Continue with Google" button), Resend (sending emails), an AI service (marking your writing) and our web hosting company. They only receive what they need to do their job.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your account and everything saved with it. Email us and we will do it.
  • You can stop using the site at any time.

Data sources

Country, state and city lists are from the CountryStateCity database, used under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Children

The site is meant for people preparing for IELTS. If you are under 13, please use it with a parent or teacher.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
